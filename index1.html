Phillip Lisomona

Phillip Lisomona is a renowned gospel musician of the Seventh-day Adventist Church based in Lusaka, Zambia.

Beyond music ministry, he is a music producer and entrepreneur with a passion for creativity, excellence and using music to inspire, encourage and spread the message of hope.

His passion is to create meaningful gospel music that connects people with God through worship, praise and inspiration.
