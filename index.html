A little about the artist

Music with a message. A life with purpose.

Phillip Lisomona is a gospel musician of the Seventh-day Adventist Church, based in Lusaka, Zambia. As a musician and music producer, he is passionate about using music to share the message of faith, hope and encouragement.

Beyond music, Phillip is also an entrepreneur, combining creativity, dedication and purpose in his work.
