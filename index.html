About The Artist

Phillip Lisomona is a renowned gospel musician of the Seventh-day Adventist Church based in Lusaka, Zambia.

He is a gospel artist, music producer and entrepreneur passionate about using music to spread the message of hope, faith and inspiration.

Welcome to his official music platform, where you can discover albums, listen to singles and enjoy uplifting gospel music.
