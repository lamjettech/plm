About Philip

Philip Lisomona is a renowned gospel musician of the Seventh-day Adventist Church, based in Lusaka, Zambia. His music carries messages of worship, hope and faith to congregations and homes across the region.

Beyond the stage, he is a music producer and an entrepreneur, building platforms that help gospel music reach more people.
